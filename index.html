HOW TO PLAY

You are the home plate umpire. When the pitch hits the mitt, call STRIKE or BALL before the timer runs out. 3 wrong calls and you're out.

Save a miss: the wrong call that would end your game brings up a true/false quiz: STRIKE = yes, BALL = no. Answer right and the miss is taken back. You get 1 to start and +1 at level 6; Season 2 gives 1 at level 12 and +1 at level 16.

Any part of the ball touching the zone is a STRIKE. The zone's height follows the hitter (midpoint of shoulders and belt down to the knees), so a taller hitter has a taller, higher zone. The width is always the plate.

A full swing and miss is always a strike; a held check swing is not. The catcher may frame the mitt into the zone: judge where the ball crossed.

Pitchers throw lefty or righty, overhand or submarine. The dotted zone guide shows in level 1 only.

Streaks multiply your score. Edge pitches, quick calls and calls held to the last moment earn bonus.

Miss a call and ABS shows how far off it was. Press R for a slow-motion replay.

The CROWD meter is atmosphere only: boos never change a call.

Season 2 · play at first: SAFE if the runner's foot touches the base before the ball reaches the glove, otherwise OUT. In levels 12–13 the touch and the catch flash as a cue.

Season 2 · foul pole (level 16+): a ball that hits the pole or passes inside it is a HOME RUN; outside it is FOUL.
